Projects & Ventures

UCONN FIRST-YEAR ENGINEERING EXPO

Martian Exploration Bridge Design

Collaborated in the competitive First-Year Engineering Expo, where classes competed to integrate multiple distinct systems (bridge, wind turbine, rover) into a unified Martian diorama. Engineered a structural bridge concept optimized for Martian exploration, adhering to strict size constraints to ensure compatibility with other projects—specifically guaranteeing the rover could successfully navigate through the bridge. Successfully incorporated advanced stretch goals into our design, including internal LED routing and a dedicated pedestrian sidewalk.

[+] VIEW PROTOTYPES & POSTER
PROJECT POSTER
PROTOTYPE 1

Initial physical prototype utilizing styrofoam and boards to conceptualize structural supports. Configured four solar panels in a series connection to plan and test the power supply for the bridge's LED system.

PROTOTYPE 2 & CAD MODEL

Transitioned to a hybrid model based on initial SolidWorks designs. Utilized balsa wood for the side trusses to reduce 3D printing time, combined with fully 3D-printed top and bottom decks. The top deck features custom inserts allowing us to safely position the solar panels for later configurations.

On Martian Diorama
FINAL PROTOTYPE

Fully 3D-printed all components for the final iteration. With the help of my teammates, we successfully integrated a functional, solar-powered LED traffic light system. The final design incorporates all planned features, including the pedestrian sidewalk and verified structural support allowing the Martian rover to cross the bridge.
